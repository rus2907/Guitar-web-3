YOUR TEACHER

Hi, I'm Rohit Bhadane.

At RDB Chords, lessons are built around a balanced combination of technical skill, music theory, creativity, and performance training — structured to suit students of every age and skill level.

Rather than relying only on repetitive chord exercises, students learn chords, strumming patterns, and technique through the songs they already love. It's a practical, song-based approach that keeps lessons engaging while showing exactly how musical concepts apply to real playing — whether you're here for personal enjoyment, live performance, or working toward Trinity College London exams.

CURRICULUM

A curriculum for every stage

A structured pathway from first chord to graded exam, balancing technical skill, music theory, creativity, and performance training at every level.

Level 1

Beginner

A strong foundation in playing technique, rhythm exercises, and basic theory, with major and minor scale and chord formation, finger exercises, and metronome practice — kept engaging through familiar songs.

Foundation · technique & basics

Intermediate

Builds on beginner skills with picking techniques, barre chords, scale application, ear training, rhythm development, and genre-based playing styles for a more versatile guitarist.
