MEET YOUR TEACHER

Hi, I'm Rohit Bhadane.

Rohit picked up the guitar at just 12 years old, and from the very beginning he loved figuring out melodies and leads by ear — a skill that shaped his journey as a musician.

>He began learning in Goa at Master O Fernando Music Academy, then continued in Pune at Guitar Hall and at Dev’s Music Academy before moving to Chennai to study full time at Dr. A.R. Rahman’s K.M. Conservatory. (www.kmmc.in)

At K.M. Conservatory, affiliated with Middlesex University (London), he majored in Modern Acoustic Guitar under the guidance of Prof. Steeve Vatz. Later, he joined Steeve Guitar Centre in Chennai for Intense Guitar Training in both Acoustic and Electric Guitar. This is where Rohit mastered Advanced Chords, Syncopations, Soloing Techniques.

He completed “Foundation Course of Music” from Middlesex University in collaboration with K. M. Conservatory. Also completed “Level 1 to 3” of Preparatory Programme in Guitar from KMMC. He completed Trinity Grade Exams of Acoustic Guitar Grade 8. He also completed Trinity Grade Exam of Initial and Grade 1 for Classical Guitar as well.

For three years, he worked as a Music Educator at Furtados School of Music (FSM), where he trained in Teaching Methodologies and completed the Music Pedagogy Certificate Program. (MPCP)

Rohit launched rdbchords.com in Chennai — his own space to share music, teach, and inspire the next generation of guitarists

CURRICULUM

A Curriculum That Grows With You

Every student follows a clear learning pathway designed to build confidence, technical ability, creativity, and musicianship — one step at a time.

Level 1

Beginner

Develop a strong musical foundation through proper technique, rhythm training, finger exercises, open chords, scales, music theory, and complete beginner-friendly songs.

Focus · Technique · Rhythm · Confidence
Level 2

Intermediate

Strengthen your playing with barre chords, fingerstyle, alternate picking, scales, improvisation, ear training, rhythm development, and genre-based playing.

Focus · Versatility · Expression · Musical Growth
Level 3

Advanced

Refine advanced techniques, speed, phrasing, improvisation, harmony, dynamics, and performance skills to develop your own musical voice.

Focus · Mastery · Creativity · Performance
Exam pathway

Trinity College London

Students interested in internationally recognized qualifications can follow the official Trinity College London guitar syllabus. Lessons include technical work, repertoire, sight reading, aural skills, music theory, and complete preparation for graded examinations.

Available · as an optional learning pathway
